Atapi Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Who uses Atapi

Atapi is a personal scheduling app operated by its owner for their own use. It is not available to the public.

Google data Atapi can access

Atapi requests one Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.app.created. This lets Atapi create its own secondary calendar ("Timeboxes") and manage events on calendars it created. Atapi cannot read any other calendar, and it has no access to email, contacts, files or any other Google data.

How the data is used

Atapi uses this access only to create, update and remove planned work sessions on the Timeboxes calendar for its owner.

To plan the schedule, event titles and times may be sent to the AI model provider that powers Atapi, solely to provide this scheduling feature. Atapi does not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Storage and security

Atapi runs on its owner's own computer. Its Google access token is stored on that computer and is not shared.

Sharing

Atapi does not sell Google user data, share it for advertising, or transfer it to anyone except as described above.

Atapi's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the Timeboxes calendar deletes all events Atapi created.
